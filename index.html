One partner for airport transfers across Sicily

We are based at Catania Airport and work across the whole of Sicily, with more than 5,000 transfers since 2022. Flights are tracked in real time, and your driver is at the pickup point 15 minutes before the agreed time. Frequently asked questions

Do you offer fixed prices for Catania Airport transfers? + −

Yes. All our Catania Airport transfers are quoted in advance with fixed prices, including tolls and standard waiting time (VAT included for private clients; agency rates are net of VAT). Night and holiday supplements, where they apply, are shown in the quote, so nothing is added after the ride. For agencies, an optional fuel adjustment tied to the official monthly diesel average works in both directions.

How do you handle delayed or early flights? + −

We monitor flight status in real time. Your chauffeur adjusts the pick‑up time accordingly and waits inside the arrivals hall within the agreed waiting window, so your guests are always met even when the schedule changes.

Can you support groups and events of different sizes? + −

Yes. We arrange sedans, vans and buses for anything from a small executive meeting to a large event. For large events, one or more coordinators follow the operations on the day and do nothing else.

Do you provide child seats if required? + −

Child and infant seats are provided free of charge on request at the time of booking. Let us know the age and number of children so we can assign the correct equipment and vehicle type.

Are you available 24/7? + −

During every service, yes: our emergency line (phone and WhatsApp) stays open from pickup to drop-off. The office is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00, and we can run services at any hour, day or night, with advance booking and subject to availability.

Prefer to talk first?

Call us, or write on WhatsApp or by email. We usually reply to messages within the hour during office hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 Italian time.
