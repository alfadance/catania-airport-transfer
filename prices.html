What the price includes

  • Mercedes-Benz E-Class for the Business Sedan, Mercedes-Benz V-Class or equivalent for the Business Minivan.
  • Tolls and VAT. Nothing is added after the ride.
  • Flight tracking. With a flight number, free waiting is 60 minutes after landing. Without it, it is 15 minutes.
  • A return trip booked together with the outbound trip costs 20% less than a single trip.

Book at least 24 hours before pickup. For less notice, message us on WhatsApp. A booking is a request until it is paid: we reply by email with a payment link.
