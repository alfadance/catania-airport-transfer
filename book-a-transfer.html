Questions from travellers

When is my booking confirmed?

When you have paid. Until then it is only a request. We send a second email when the booking is confirmed.

How long does the payment link work?

For 24 hours. If you do not pay in that time, the request is closed. You can send a new one.

My route is not in the list.

Write to us on WhatsApp or by email at info@cataniaairporttransfer.net. We work across the whole of Sicily.

What if my flight is late?

Give us your flight number. We monitor flight status in real time, and your chauffeur adjusts the pick-up time accordingly.
