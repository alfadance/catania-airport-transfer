Frequently asked questions

Are your prices fixed?

Yes. Prices are quoted in advance and include tolls and standard waiting time, with VAT included for private clients. Any night or holiday supplement is shown in the quote.

What if my flight is late?

Give us your flight number and we follow it in real time. Your chauffeur adjusts the pickup time, and free waiting is 60 minutes after landing. Without a flight number it is 15 minutes.

When is my booking confirmed?

When you have paid. We reply to your request with a payment link that works for 24 hours, then send a second email to confirm.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, free of charge on request when you book. Tell us the age and number of children.

Can you handle groups and events?

Yes. We arrange sedans, vans and buses for anything from a small meeting to a large event.

How does hourly hire work?

The minimum is 3 hours, with 20 km included per hour. Hourly hire must start and end at the same place. Extra time and distance cost €60 per hour and €0.95 per km in a Sedan, or €70 per hour and €1.15 per km in a Minivan. For a one-way trip, please book a transfer.

Do you work outside Sicily?

No. We are based at Catania Airport and work across the whole of Sicily.
