Prices are for the whole car, VAT included, for 8 hours and 160 km a day, starting from Catania Airport. From Catania city the day rate is €480 in the Business Sedan and €570 in the Business Minivan: we send the exact total in our reply. How is the price built?

  1. Each day is priced like our chauffeur by the hour: €460 for 8 hours and 160 km in the Business Sedan, €560 in the Business Minivan. The hours and km of the day count for all the driving of that day, between towns too. A day shorter or longer than 8 hours is priced case by case, up or down.
  2. Each night the driver spends away from Catania adds €150, with no discount.
  3. From the third consecutive day, the day rate is cheaper: 5% off on day 3, then two points more each day, up to 15% from day 8.
  4. Hours and km beyond those booked are charged: €60 per hour and €1.00 per km in the Business Sedan, €70 per hour and €1.50 per km in the Business Minivan.

Can I start and end anywhere in Sicily?

You can start from any town in Sicily and end in any other. Your itinerary is free: you can move between towns every day, and you do not have to come back to where you started. If the trip does not start or end in Catania or at Catania Airport, we add a positioning charge for the car to reach your start point and, at the end, to go back to Catania. We tell you the amount in our reply, before you pay.

Who is the driver?

The same English-speaking chauffeur drives you every day of the trip, unless a case of force majeure prevents the driver from carrying out the service. The driver is not a licensed guide: your driver suggests places and tells you about each stop, and you decide the route each day.

What is included?

  • A private car with driver for the hours you book each day: Mercedes-Benz E-Class (Business Sedan) or V-Class or equivalent (Business Minivan)
  • Tolls, standard waiting time and VAT
  • The driver's meals and accommodation, in the €150 for each night away from Catania
  • Child seats on request, free of charge

Not included: your own hotels, meals, entrance fees, a licensed guide.

How do I book?

  1. Send us your dates, start point, number of travellers and the places you would like to see, by WhatsApp or email, at least 24 hours before the first day. For less notice, message us on WhatsApp.
  2. We check availability and reply by email with the price and a payment link. The link works for 24 hours.
  3. You pay in advance. Your booking is confirmed once you have paid.
  4. Your driver meets you at the start point on day one.

Questions about a private driver in Sicily

How much does a private driver in Sicily cost for a day?

From €460 for 8 hours and 160 km in a Business Sedan from Catania Airport, VAT included (€480 from Catania city). A Business Minivan for up to 7 travellers is €560 from the airport (€570 from the city).

Is the same driver with me every day?

Yes, the same chauffeur drives you for the whole trip, unless a case of force majeure prevents the driver from carrying out the service.

Can I start and end the trip in different towns?

Yes. Start in any town in Sicily and end in any other. If the trip does not start or end in Catania or at Catania Airport, we add a positioning charge for the car; we tell you the amount before you pay.

What does a night away cost?

€150 per night the driver spends away from Catania, for the driver's meals and accommodation. You book your own hotel.

Is there a discount for longer trips?

Yes, from the third consecutive day: 5% off the day rate on day 3, two points more each day, up to 15% from day 8. Nights are not discounted.

Can I book days of different lengths?

Yes. A day of 8 hours is priced as above. For a day shorter or longer than 8 hours we calculate the price case by case, up or down. Tell us in your request.

Is the driver a guide?

No. The driver suggests places and tells you about each stop, but is not a licensed guide.

Is there a maximum number of days?

No. Tell us your dates and we send the price.

What if I want to change my plan during the trip?

You decide the route each day. Extra hours and km are charged at the rates above.

What happens if I cancel?

If you cancel a day more than 24 hours before it starts, we refund it. If you cancel less than 24 hours before a day starts, you pay 100% of that service: the day, the night away already spent and the positioning drive if the car has already left. The other days cost nothing.
