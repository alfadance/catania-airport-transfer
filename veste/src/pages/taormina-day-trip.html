What you can see

  • Castelmola, a hilltop village 529 m above sea level, above Taormina.
  • The Ancient Theatre, built in the 3rd century BC and rebuilt by the Romans. The visit takes less than an hour.
  • Corso Umberto, the pedestrian main street, about 800 metres between Porta Messina and Porta Catania, lined with shops and cafés.
  • Piazza IX Aprile, a terrace square that looks over the Ionian Sea and Etna.
  • Piazza Duomo, with the Baroque fountain in front of the cathedral.
  • The Villa Comunale, a quiet public garden with trees and sea views.
  • Isola Bella, a short walk from Mazzarò bay. The cable car from Taormina goes down to Mazzarò, and a flight of steps also leads to the beach. A narrow sandbar joins the islet to the shore.

These stops are suggestions. Opening times and ticket prices change, so check them on the day.

Questions about a day trip to Taormina

How long is the drive from Catania to Taormina?

Up to about 56 minutes from either Catania city or Catania Airport, on a typical weekday. Taormina is 54 km from the city and 68 km from the airport.

Is Taormina a day trip from Catania?

Yes. With up to 56 minutes of driving each way, a five-hour day leaves about three hours in Taormina and Castelmola, before any detour. A six or eight-hour day leaves more time, with Isola Bella.

How much does a private driver cost for a day in Taormina?

From €300 for five hours and 100 km for the whole car, up to 3 travellers, from Catania city or airport. A Business Minivan for up to 7 travellers costs from €350 from the airport and €360 from the city. The round trip to Taormina is about 109 km from the city and 137 km from the airport, so extra km are charged at €1.00 (sedan) or €1.50 (minivan). An eight-hour day, from €460, includes 160 km.

Is this a taxi or a tour?

It is neither. It is a private car with a licensed chauffeur (NCC) at your disposal: you book in advance, the price is fixed for the hours and km you book, and you decide where to go.

Is the driver a guide?

No. The driver tells you about each stop but is not a licensed guide.

Can I change the stops?

Yes. The stops above are suggestions, and you decide the pace and the order. More stops and detours add time and km, and what goes beyond the booked hours and included km is charged.
