Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Catania Airport Transfer (Catania Airport Transfer di Valenti Sebastiano, Via Badia 3, 95123 Catania (CT), Italy, VAT IT05924950875, REA: CT-448461). We provide B2B chauffeur and airport transfer services in Sicily.

Data we collect

When you contact us via email, phone, WhatsApp or any form on this site, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, company name and the content of your message. We use this information only to respond to your enquiries, provide quotes and manage bookings.

How we use your data

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing. We may retain contact details and correspondence for the time necessary to fulfil contracts, handle disputes and comply with legal obligations.

Private bookings

If you book a transfer as a private traveller, the booking form asks for the route, date and time, passengers, luggage, flight number, address, name, phone number and email. The form sends these details to our booking system. We use them to prepare your quote, to run the transfer and to contact you about it. We keep the request for as long as we keep the record of the service. We keep that record for the time necessary to fulfil the contract, handle disputes and comply with legal obligations.

When you choose a town on the booking page, the page shows a map of the route. Your browser then loads map tiles from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), which receives your IP address and the area of the map you view. We send it no other data, and we do not load the map before you choose a town.

We take card payments through Stripe. When you pay, you enter your card details on Stripe's secure page, not on our site, and we never see or keep your full card number. Stripe tells us that the payment went through and gives us the amount and a payment reference. We keep these with your booking, to run the transfer and for our accounts. Stripe handles your payment data under its own privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

If we wrote to your company first

We sometimes introduce our services to travel agencies, tour operators and DMCs that sell Sicily. We only write to business email addresses that the company publishes on its own website, and we note where we found them. We do this on the basis of our legitimate interest in offering our services to businesses (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If a named person replies, we keep their name, business email and our correspondence only to continue the conversation. If you reply "no thanks", or ask us by email, we stop writing and keep only your company name and address on a do-not-contact list, so that we do not write again. Companies that do not reply are removed from our list after 24 months.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses technical cookies, and Google Analytics 4 cookies only if you accept them in the cookie banner, to understand how the site is used. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie preferences" at the bottom of the home page. No sensitive personal data is stored in cookies.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, or object to its processing, by contacting us at info@cataniaairporttransfer.net. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are in the EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when changes are made. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
