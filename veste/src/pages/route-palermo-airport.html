Looking for a taxi from Catania Airport to Palermo Airport? This is a private transfer with a licensed chauffeur (NCC), not a street taxi: you book in advance, the price is fixed and we follow your flight.

Palermo Airport (PMO) is the main airport in the west of Sicily. This transfer is useful if you land in Catania and fly home from Palermo, or the other way round.

It is one of the longest transfers we offer: about 2 hours 20 minutes by car. Plan the pick-up with a margin if you have a flight to catch, and add your flight number to the booking.

Book at least 24 hours before pickup. Add your flight number in the form: we follow your flight, and with a flight number free waiting is 60 minutes after landing.

Questions about this transfer

How long is the drive from Catania Airport to Palermo Airport?

About 138 minutes by car (216 km). The time is calculated for a typical weekday with usual traffic in the morning and in the afternoon, and we keep the longer one.

How much does the transfer to Palermo Airport cost?

One price for the whole vehicle, VAT included, however many passengers travel.

What if my flight is late?

Give us your flight number. We monitor flight status in real time, and your chauffeur adjusts the pick-up time accordingly. With a flight number, free waiting is 60 minutes after landing. Without it, it is 15 minutes.

When is my booking confirmed?

When you have paid. Until then it is only a request. We send a second email when the booking is confirmed. The payment link works for 24 hours.

Can I book the way back from Palermo Airport to the airport?

Yes. Choose "To Catania Airport" in the booking form.
