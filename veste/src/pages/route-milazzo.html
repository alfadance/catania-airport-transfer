Questions about this transfer

How long is the drive from Catania Airport to Milazzo?

About 103 minutes by car (145 km). The time is calculated for a typical weekday with usual traffic in the morning and in the afternoon, and we keep the longer one.

How much does the transfer to Milazzo cost?

One price for the whole vehicle, VAT included, however many passengers travel.

What if my flight is late?

Give us your flight number. We monitor flight status in real time, and your chauffeur adjusts the pick-up time accordingly. With a flight number, free waiting is 60 minutes after landing. Without it, it is 15 minutes.

When is my booking confirmed?

When you have paid. Until then it is only a request. We send a second email when the booking is confirmed. The payment link works for 24 hours.

Can I book the way back from Milazzo to the airport?

Yes. Choose "To Catania Airport" in the booking form.
