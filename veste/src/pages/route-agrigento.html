Looking for a taxi from Catania Airport to Agrigento? This is a private transfer with a licensed chauffeur (NCC), not a street taxi: you book in advance, the price is fixed and we follow your flight.

Agrigento is best known for the Valley of the Temples, a UNESCO World Heritage site with Greek temples on a ridge facing the sea.

It is a long transfer: about 1 hour 40 minutes by car. Many guests break the journey, or book it as a one-way transfer between the east and the south-west of Sicily.

Book at least 24 hours before pickup. Add your flight number in the form: we follow your flight, and with a flight number free waiting is 60 minutes after landing.

Questions about this transfer

How long is the drive from Catania Airport to Agrigento?

About 102 minutes by car (158 km). The time is calculated for a typical weekday with usual traffic in the morning and in the afternoon, and we keep the longer one.

How much does the transfer to Agrigento cost?

One price for the whole vehicle, VAT included, however many passengers travel.

What if my flight is late?

Give us your flight number. We monitor flight status in real time, and your chauffeur adjusts the pick-up time accordingly. With a flight number, free waiting is 60 minutes after landing. Without it, it is 15 minutes.

When is my booking confirmed?

When you have paid. Until then it is only a request. We send a second email when the booking is confirmed. The payment link works for 24 hours.

Can I book the way back from Agrigento to the airport?

Yes. Choose "To Catania Airport" in the booking form.
